A story of your year with AI coding tools, made from the logs they already keep on your machine.

npx ai-wrapped-year

Needs Node 22.13 or newer.

Reads the tools you use

  • Claude Code
  • Codex
  • Cursor
  • Antigravity
  • Grok
  • Copilot
  • Amp
  • Gemini CLI
  • opencode

Every profile and account on the machine counts once. Using something else? npx ai-wrapped-year --doctor shows what it found.

Compare with friends

Every wrap has a friend code: the year, a persona and five totals. Paste a friend's code into your wrap, or run:

npx ai-wrapped-year --vs sam=aw1-1ka.1.3e8.1kqsbm8.2s.e.9ix

Stays on your machine

  • No sign-in and nothing to upload.
  • Prompts and replies are never stored, printed or sent.
  • The page it makes is one file with no network requests.

Only two options reach the network, and only when you pass them: --github and --compare.
